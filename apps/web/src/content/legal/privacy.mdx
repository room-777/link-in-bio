Grabbin ("Grabbin", "we", "us", or "our") provides tools for creating and sharing link-in-bio pages. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [support@grabbin.me](mailto:support@grabbin.me).

## Information we collect

### Account profile information

The account profile information we store is limited to:

- your name;
- your email address; and
- your profile image, if you provide one.

We do not ask for or maintain a separate profile containing your phone number, address, company name, or payment card number.

You may sign in with email verification codes or with Google or X/Twitter. If you use a social sign-in provider, that provider may share the account information you authorize it to share with us.

To provide authentication and connected features, we may also process service records such as login session information, including IP address and browser user-agent, email-verification records, and identifiers or access tokens for sign-in or connected providers when needed. These records are separate from your account profile.

### Pages and content

We collect the information you add to your pages, including handles, names, bios, profile images, links, text, media, map locations, layout settings, and other page settings. A published page and its published content are public and may be viewed by anyone with the page URL.

### Billing information

Paid plans are processed through Creem, our payment provider and merchant of record. Creem may process your name, email address, billing address, payment details, and order information under its own terms and privacy policy. To connect a purchase to your Grabbin account, we store only the Creem customer ID, Creem subscription ID, and Creem order ID. We do not store your full payment card number in Grabbin.

### Communications

If you contact us, we may receive your email address and the contents of your message, including files or other information you choose to send. We also use your email address to send sign-in codes and account-deletion confirmation messages.

### Usage and technical information

Our infrastructure and service providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request times, referring pages, and error information.

Grabbin uses Simple Analytics for page-view reporting. We manually send page-view events, including the current route and a stable identifier for a public Grabbin page when that page's view statistics are enabled. We do not use Grabbin analytics for advertising or sell analytics data.

Simple Analytics does not use cookies or store IP addresses for this page-view reporting.

### Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and similar technologies for authentication and security. Simple Analytics page-view reporting does not use cookies. We do not use advertising cookies on Grabbin. Third-party services may use their own technologies when you use their services or follow links to them; their own privacy policies apply.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and maintain accounts;
- host, display, save, and synchronize your pages and content;
- send sign-in, account-security, and account-deletion emails;
- process paid-plan access and manage subscriptions;
- provide page-view statistics when the feature is enabled;
- prevent abuse, investigate security incidents, and enforce our Terms of Service;
- respond to support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

## Public pages and your responsibility

Anything you publish on a public Grabbin page can be viewed, copied, linked to, or indexed by other people and search engines. Do not publish information that you want to keep private. You are responsible for making sure that your content is accurate, lawful, and safe to share, and that you have the rights needed to upload or link to it.

## When we share information

We share information only as needed to provide Grabbin, operate our infrastructure, or comply with the law. Our service providers include:

- **Cloudflare**, which provides hosting, edge delivery, and object storage for the service;
- **Resend**, which delivers authentication and account emails;
- **Creem**, which processes paid-plan transactions and subscription information;
- **Simple Analytics**, which provides aggregated page-view reporting;
- **Google and X/Twitter**, if you choose one of those providers to sign in; and
- **Mapbox**, when map display or location search is used.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect users or the service, or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets. We do not sell your personal information.

When you click a link or use an embedded or linked third-party service from a public page, that third party may collect information directly from you. We do not control the privacy practices of those third parties.

## Data retention and account deletion

We keep account and page information while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can start account deletion from your account settings. We send a confirmation email before deletion is completed.

After confirmation, we immediately delete your Grabbin account profile information, page content, and stored Creem identifiers from Grabbin. Uploaded media associated with deleted pages is queued for object-storage cleanup and may take up to 24 hours to be removed. Creem may retain its own payment records under its privacy policy and legal obligations.

## International processing

Grabbin and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers. By using Grabbin, you understand that your information may be processed by the providers listed in this policy in the locations where they operate.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, email [support@grabbin.me](mailto:support@grabbin.me) from the address connected to your account and describe what you need. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity. Some information may need to be kept to meet legal, security, or payment obligations.

## Children's privacy

Grabbin is a general-audience service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact [support@grabbin.me](mailto:support@grabbin.me) so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong, private email account and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our service or legal obligations change. We will post the updated version on this page and change the date at the top. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice where required.
