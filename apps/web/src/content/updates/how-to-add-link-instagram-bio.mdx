## Add the address in Instagram's profile editor

Open Instagram and go to your profile. Choose **Edit profile**, then open the area labeled **Links** or **Add external link**. Paste the complete public address, review the title, and save. Instagram may change button names or layout; use the current controls shown in your app. Meta also lets profiles include multiple external links, as described in [its announcement](https://about.fb.com/ko/news/2023/04/instagram%EC%9D%B4-%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EB%A1%9C%EC%9A%B4-%EC%9E%90%EA%B8%B0%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84%EC%9D%84-%EC%84%A0%EB%B3%B4/).

## Choose a direct link or a page

Use a direct link when you want every visitor to reach one specific destination, such as an event, a new article, or a portfolio. Use a separate link page when visitors need to choose between several projects or understand your work before opening another site.

Do not add a page just because other creators use one. The extra step should give visitors something useful: a clearer choice, a short introduction, or media that explains what they will find.

## Test the route before sharing

After saving, open your profile as a visitor and tap the link. Check that:

- The address opens without requiring you to be signed in.
- The destination matches what your bio or post promised.
- The page is readable on a phone.
- Every next-step link opens the right site.

If a social post promotes one current item, consider making that destination the first choice. Review it again when the promotion ends so people do not land on an expired page.
