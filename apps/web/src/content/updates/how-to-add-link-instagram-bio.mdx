## How to add a link to your Instagram bio

1. Open Instagram and go to your profile.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap **Links**.
4. Tap **Add external link**, paste the full public address, and add a title if you want one.
5. Save. You can repeat this for up to five links.

Button names can change between app versions; use the controls your app shows. Meta's [announcement of multiple profile links](https://about.fb.com/ko/news/2023/04/instagram%EC%9D%B4-%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EB%A1%9C%EC%9A%B4-%EC%9E%90%EA%B8%B0%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84%EC%9D%84-%EC%84%A0%EB%B3%B4/) describes the feature. Links you add here do not use any of your bio's [150 characters](/update/instagram-bio-character-limit).

## Choose a direct link or a page

Use a direct link when you want every visitor to reach one destination, such as an event, a new article, or a portfolio. Use a link page when visitors need to choose between several projects or understand your work before opening another site. ## Test the route before sharing

After saving, open your profile as a visitor and tap each link. Check that:

- The address opens without requiring you to be signed in.
- The destination matches what your bio or post promised.
- The page is readable on a phone.
- Every next-step link opens the right site.

If people book time with you, a [Calendly widget](/update/calendly-widget) on your page lets them pick a slot without leaving it.

## Can people see when you click their link on Instagram?

No. Instagram does not notify anyone when you tap a link on their profile. Professional accounts can see the total number of link taps in Insights, but not who tapped. For what you can and cannot measure, see [how to track Instagram bio link clicks](/update/track-instagram-bio-link-clicks).
