## Start with the page you want visitors to see

People searching for a Linktree alternative may want a different layout, a place for selected work, or a simpler way to present several parts of their online identity. Those are different needs. Decide which one matters before comparing tools.

Instagram now supports up to five profile links, so a separate page is not necessary for everyone. A single landing page can still help when you want one address to introduce your work visually and point people to several destinations. [Meta's announcement explains the native link option](https://about.fb.com/ko/news/2023/04/instagram%EC%9D%B4-%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EB%A1%9C%EC%9A%B4-%EC%9E%90%EA%B8%B0%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84%EC%9D%84-%EC%84%A0%EB%B3%B4/).

## Compare the parts that change a visitor's experience

Look at four things in the actual published page:

1. **Presentation:** Is it a short list, a full website, or a flexible visual page?
2. **Content:** Can you show the media and context visitors need, or only link labels?
3. **Next step:** Does a visitor know which link to open or how to contact you?
4. **Ownership:** Can you use a stable address that you are comfortable sharing?

Then check practical limits: what the free plan includes, whether the page carries platform branding, how custom domains work, and whether the service offers capabilities you actually need. Do not pay for analytics or store tools if your main goal is to show a few projects and direct people to existing destinations.

## Match the format to your work

A musician promoting one new release may be well served by a clean link list. A photographer may need a strong image, a small set of galleries, and links to existing booking or contact pages. A freelancer may need a short service description beside selected work. A creator with a large catalogue may need a full site or a storefront.

Grabbin is for people who want to arrange links, photos, videos, text, and places as visual blocks on one shareable page. It is not a booking form, online store, or replacement for a full portfolio site. Keep those functions on the services already built for them and link to them clearly.

## Test before you switch

Build a small draft before moving every profile link. Open it on a phone, tap each destination, and ask someone unfamiliar with your work to find one important item. If they hesitate, improve the labels or order.
