## Where links go on your YouTube channel

YouTube gives you two main places for links. **Channel profile links** are added in YouTube Studio under the channel's profile or basic info settings, and appear on your channel page. **Video descriptions** can carry links relevant to that video, such as the products, sources, or playlists you mention. YouTube's settings and eligibility rules change, so check the current [YouTube Help Center](https://support.google.com/youtube) before relying on a placement.

## Why one link page helps

Your channel, other social profiles, a newsletter, merch, and business contact all compete for the same few link slots. One page can hold them in a clear order, so a viewer arriving from Instagram, TikTok, or a video description sees the most relevant next step first. You update the page once instead of editing every profile and description.

## What to put on it

- **A featured video or series.** "Start with the travel series" tells a new viewer more than a link called "YouTube".
- **Your channel link** for people who want the full library.
- **One sentence of context** about your subject, format, or audience.
- **Other platforms** only when they support the viewer's next decision.
- **A separate business contact** for sponsorships and collaborations, using an email or form you already manage.

Too many equal links make the channel harder to explore, so keep the list short.

## Setup steps

1. Build the page with your featured video, channel link, and contact destination.
2. Open it on a phone and check that every video and link opens as expected.
3. Add the page address to your channel profile links in YouTube Studio.
4. Use the same address in your other profiles, such as Instagram ([how to add a link to your Instagram bio](/update/how-to-add-link-instagram-bio)) and TikTok ([how to add Instagram to your TikTok bio](/update/how-to-add-instagram-to-tiktok-bio)).
5. Review the featured item when you publish a new series.
