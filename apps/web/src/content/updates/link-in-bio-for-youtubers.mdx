## Give a new viewer a reason to choose a video

A YouTube creator's link-in-bio page should lead with the video or series most relevant to the audience arriving from another profile. A link called “YouTube” is clear, but a label like “Start with the travel series” tells a first-time visitor more when that playlist exists.

Keep a channel link for people who want the full library. Add a featured video, current series, or other platform only if it supports the viewer's next decision. Too many equal links make the channel harder to explore.

## Add context that a thumbnail cannot carry

Use one sentence to explain the subject, format, or audience of a featured series. If the page contains video, verify that it plays or opens as expected on a phone. A short description can also help returning viewers see what changed since their last visit.

## Separate viewing from business inquiries

If you receive sponsorship or collaboration requests, label the professional contact destination separately from public videos. Use a business email or form you already manage. Do not imply that a page builder screens inquiries or negotiates deals.
