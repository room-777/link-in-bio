## A little corner of the internet that's yours

Sometimes you want more than a page at someone else's address. You want a little corner of the internet that feels like yours—an address you can put on a card, share with friends, and keep using as your work or interests grow.

That's why custom domains are part of Pro. Your page can have an address you chose, making it feel more personal and easier for people to remember.

## Who can use it

Custom domains are available to Pro members who have a domain of their own. Only subdomains, such as `links.example.com`, can be connected. This helps us keep the service consistent and reliable.

## How to get started

Open your page settings and choose **Custom domain**. Enter the address you'd like to use, then follow the steps shown. Once it's ready, share it with your audience. Your original page link will keep working too.

## A note about editing

Your custom domain is for viewing your page. To edit it, open the page at your existing handle address and make your changes there.

You can manage or disconnect your custom domain from the same settings whenever you need to.
