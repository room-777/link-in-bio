## Use TikTok's social profile setting

Open TikTok, go to **Profile**, select **Edit profile**, and look for the option to add a social account. Choose Instagram and follow the sign-in and connection prompts shown by TikTok. The setting may not appear for every account or app version, so rely on the options available in your own profile.

TikTok's [profile setup help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account) explains its social-account linking feature. The process can differ from adding a website address; check the screen label before choosing a field.

## Confirm the connection from the public view

Return to the profile and check whether the Instagram icon or destination is visible. If it is missing, make sure the app is current, the accounts were connected successfully, and TikTok has not displayed an account-specific restriction. Do not repeatedly disconnect and reconnect without checking the message in the app.

## One social link or a shared page?

Connecting Instagram is useful when the goal is to move between social profiles. It does not explain your work or organize your other destinations. A separate page can help when people should choose between a portfolio, a release, a newsletter, and a contact route.

Keep each destination labeled in plain words. A page should reduce uncertainty, not add a second list that repeats the same information. Grabbin can combine photos, videos, text, places, and links in one visual page; your booking, shop, or full website can remain at its current destination.
