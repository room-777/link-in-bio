Make scheduling easier with the Calendly widget. Pro members can show their event types and available times on Grabbin, so visitors can find a time that works for them and book through Calendly. Choose from five presets to find a layout that fits.

## How to get started

Connect your Calendly account in **Account Settings**. Then add the Calendly widget in Grabbin and choose which event to share.

Visitors can browse available times and complete their booking through Calendly.
