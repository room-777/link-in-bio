## A social-account link and a website link are different settings

TikTok lets users link certain social accounts from the profile editor. A website address is a separate option, and its availability may depend on account eligibility or location. Do not assume that connecting Instagram also creates a clickable website link.

TikTok's [official profile setup page](https://support.tiktok.com/en/getting-started/setting-up-your-profile/linking-another-social-media-account?lang=hi) says a website link is available to accounts with at least 1,000 followers or a Registered Business Account. Check the current help page and your app because platform rules can change.

## Check the profile editor

Open **Profile**, choose **Edit profile**, and look for the website field. If it appears, enter the complete public page address and save. If it does not appear, read TikTok's current eligibility guidance rather than trying an unrelated social-link field.

After saving, open your public profile and tap the link. Confirm that it loads without sign-in and that the first destination matches your profile description.

## Make the destination useful on a phone

Someone arriving from a short video usually wants the relevant item quickly. Put the video project, product page, or full portfolio near the top. Use short labels and remove expired promotions. If the profile link leads to a page with several choices, keep the page order aligned with the content the visitor just watched.
