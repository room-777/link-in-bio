## Edit the bio from your profile

Open Instagram and visit your profile. Select **Edit profile**, change the bio text, and save. The exact button labels may vary by app version. If you want to change your website destination, open the profile's **Links** section and edit the appropriate link there; the bio text field and profile-link controls are separate.

## Rewrite the line before you replace it

Before editing, decide what changed. Is your role different? Are you promoting a new project? Do you want people to contact you another way? Keep the sentence focused on the detail a new visitor needs now.

An easy draft pattern is: “I [make/do] [specific work]. See [current destination] below.” Keep only details that are accurate. Avoid leaving an availability note, event date, or launch link in place after it is no longer current.

## Check both text and links

After saving, open the public profile and review the line breaks. Then tap the profile link. A bio update does not always change your link destination, so check both separately. If you use multiple profile links, make sure the most useful option is easy to identify.

When your profile points to a landing page, open each destination from that page too. A good bio link is not finished until the full path works on a phone.
