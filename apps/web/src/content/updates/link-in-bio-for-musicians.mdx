## Put the current release first

A musician's link-in-bio page should make the current listening path easy to find. Lead with the release, performance video, or event that matters now. Give the link a direct label such as “Listen to the new EP” or “Watch the live session” when that is what it opens.

Keep an archive or “all releases” destination for listeners who want to explore older work. Do not make a visitor choose among several unlabeled streaming icons without explaining what is different.

## Group the page around listener intent

A practical order can be:

1. Short artist or project introduction.
2. Current release or featured video.
3. Other streaming destinations.
4. Upcoming shows or an event page, if current.
5. Press, social profiles, or contact details.

Remove a date after the event has passed. If ticket sales or merchandise are handled elsewhere, link to that service and make the destination clear.

## Choose a page format that suits your catalog

If you promote one song at a time, a direct release link may be the shortest route. If you want to show videos, photos, and several projects together, a visual page can add context. A music distribution or storefront service remains the right place for tasks it already supports.
