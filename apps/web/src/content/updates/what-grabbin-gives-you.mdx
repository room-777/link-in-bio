## Everything you share, in one page

Grabbin gives you one flexible page for the links, photos, videos, places, and notes you want people to find. Add each piece as a block, then arrange the page around what matters to you.

## Choose a size that fits

Links, media, maps, and text can each use different layout sizes, so you can give important content more room and keep smaller details compact. Section blocks stay full-width to keep page headings easy to scan.

## Add the blocks you need

Each preview below is an interactive example. Try the controls and content directly in the examples.

### Links with useful context

Add a link and show more than its URL. Provider details, such as an Instagram profile and follower count, can make a link easier to recognize at a glance.

## Preview and edit the mobile layout from your computer

You can switch between desktop and mobile layouts while working on your computer. Preview how your page looks on a phone, then edit that layout directly—there is no need to pick up your phone to make changes.

## Share with a QR code or a link

When your page is ready, share a QR code people can scan or copy the link to send anywhere.
