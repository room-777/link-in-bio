### Why I loved Bento

I've wanted to make my own link-in-bio page for a long time. I used Bento, and when it shut down, I spent a lot of time looking for another service.

Bento's editing experience was the best I had tried. Its interface felt carefully considered, I could edit my whole page in one place, and the Bento-style layout made it stand out from other tools.

### Why I wanted an alternative

Linktree is a good tool, but I wanted to make something that suited me better. Its editing experience didn't work for me. It also had features I didn't need, and customizing a page required a paid plan. For what I wanted, that plan didn't feel worth the price.

After Bento closed, many similar services appeared, and I tried them myself. Some had the Bento-style layout, but I couldn't find the same care in their interface and editing experience. As a free user, I also ran into a lot of feature limits.

### How it came to be

That's why I started building Grabbin. I've lost count of how many hours I've spent getting here. I built and scrapped five different versions because none felt right.

I gave up more than once, but the idea kept coming back. After several attempts, Grabbin is finally here.

### What I want it to be

I want Grabbin to focus on what a link-in-bio service needs to do and make every part of the experience feel carefully considered. I don't know if you'll enjoy using it as much as I enjoy making it, but I care about getting the details right.

Grabbin is free to use, and you can add unlimited images and videos. I want the free plan to include what you need to build and use your page without feeling held back.

### The future

I can't predict where Grabbin will go. It may be forgotten, or it may bring together the people I've imagined using it.

I plan to keep adding widgets and features, including an all-in-one calendar and custom domains, and help Grabbin find its place among link-in-bio services. I hope more people will join. Even if they don't, I love this service and plan to keep building it for myself.
