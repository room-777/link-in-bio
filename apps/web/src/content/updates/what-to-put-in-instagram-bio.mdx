## Include the information that helps a new visitor

When deciding what to put in an Instagram bio, use a short order: identify yourself, describe the work, and point to one next step. The visitor should not need to infer your role from a list of hashtags or several unrelated links.

For example, a freelance illustrator might include a role, a specialty, and a portfolio destination. A musician might name the project and point to the latest release. These are structures, not ready-made claims; use details that describe your actual work.

## Pick one detail that makes the description specific

“Designer” is accurate but broad. Add a type of work or audience when it helps: “Product designer focused on accessible mobile tools.” A photographer might name portrait work or a region if those details help visitors decide whether to explore further.

Use only claims you can support. If you mention awards, years of experience, client results, or availability, make sure they are current and true. Clear plain words are more useful than a long string of promotional adjectives.

## Use the link for everything that does not fit

Your bio does not need to contain every social profile, project, and contact option. Instagram supports multiple profile links, and a separate page can help when you want to show projects alongside their destinations. Meta describes the native profile-link feature [here](https://about.fb.com/ko/news/2023/04/instagram%EC%9D%B4-%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EB%A1%9C%EC%9A%B4-%EC%9E%90%EA%B8%B0%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84%EC%9D%84-%EC%84%A0%EB%B3%B4/).

Grabbin can combine links with photos, videos, short text, and places. Keep a detailed portfolio or booking process on its own service and link to it rather than squeezing it into profile copy. See [Instagram bio ideas](/update/instagram-bio-ideas) and [the visual page guide](/update/build-visual-link-in-bio).

## Read it as a stranger

Open your profile without explaining it. Can you tell who the account is for and what to do next? If not, remove a secondary detail and make the main action clearer.
