## Begin with one reason for the page

A personal website can introduce your work, collect projects, publish writing, or point people to your other profiles. Choose one main job before filling it with sections. When every part of your online life appears at equal weight, visitors have to decide what matters for themselves.

## Useful page ideas by goal

- **Show your work:** feature a small selection and link to detailed projects.
- **Share writing:** put a current article first, with an archive or newsletter destination nearby.
- **Introduce a creative practice:** pair a short description with images or video that show your medium.
- **Help people contact you:** state the kind of message you welcome and link to your preferred contact method.
- **Bring multiple profiles together:** name each destination so people know what they will find there.
- **Share places or field work:** include a map or location context only when it supports the story.

These are page structures, not claims about what every creator needs. Remove any section that does not help a visitor understand your work or act on it.

## Keep the page maintainable

Use links to existing tools for long case studies, booking, payment, and email signup. That way, you do not have to duplicate and update those workflows. Revisit the page when a project moves, an event passes, or your availability changes.
