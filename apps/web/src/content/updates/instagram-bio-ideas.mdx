## Make the first sentence do useful work

Searches for Instagram bio ideas often lead to lists of clever lines. A line is useful only when it helps the right visitor understand the account. Start with what you make or do, then add one detail that distinguishes your focus.

Try these fill-in prompts rather than copying a generic slogan:

- “I make [type of work] for [kind of audience].”
- “Selected [projects / photos / writing] from [name or studio].”
- “[Role] exploring [subject or place]. Current work: [short project name].”
- “Available for [specific kind of work]. See recent projects below.”
- “Notes on [topic]. New writing and contact details at [destination].”

Replace bracketed text with something accurate. If you cannot say who the audience is, describe the work instead. Skip claims such as “award-winning” unless you can name and support the award.

## Choose one call to action

Tell visitors what to do next: view selected work, read the latest post, listen to a release, or contact you. If you have multiple priorities, put them on a landing page with clear labels rather than packing every request into the bio text.

Instagram can display links directly on your profile. A separate page is most helpful when it adds context or lets people browse several parts of your work. Meta's announcement about profile links is [here](https://about.fb.com/ko/news/2023/04/instagram%EC%9D%B4-%EC%9E%90%EC%9C%A0%EB%A1%9C%EC%9A%B4-%EC%9E%90%EA%B8%B0%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84%EC%9D%84-%EC%84%A0%EB%B3%B4/).

## Keep the bio and page consistent

If the bio says “portrait photographer,” lead with portrait work. If it says “ceramic artist,” show pieces or a collection before unrelated links. The profile and destination should sound like the same person and make the same promise.

For the page, a visual editor can help place media and links together. Grabbin supports text, photos, videos, places, and links as blocks. It does not create a shop or take bookings; point visitors to your existing service when needed. See the [Instagram link setup guide](/update/link-in-bio-instagram) and [ideas for a personal website](/update/personal-website-ideas).

Read your bio aloud. Remove filler, check the destination, and update it when your focus changes.
