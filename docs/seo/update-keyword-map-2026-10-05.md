# Grabbin Update Keyword Map — October 5, 2026

## Scope and limits

Market: United States, English. Product fit: Grabbin is a visual personal page builder for creators and freelancers. It combines links, photos, videos, text, and places in a flexible layout. It does not provide built-in booking, inquiry forms, checkout, or a full portfolio archive.

Ahrefs Free Keyword Generator was checked on October 5, 2026 for the seeds `link in bio`, `link in bio tools`, `Instagram bio`, `Linktree alternatives`, `portfolio website`, and `personal website`. The free tool showed the first 20 ideas per seed, US volume bands, and a difficulty label where available. `>1,000`, `>100`, `<100`, and `>10,000` below are the tool's displayed bands, not exact monthly counts. The visible KD labels `Easy`, `Medium`, and `Hard` are reproduced as shown; a blank/unknown label is not treated as easy.

Because phrase variants often share one intent, the 30 articles are not 30 independent high-volume opportunities. The strongest measured topics are prioritized first. Lower-volume or unmeasured role-specific pages are supporting guides based on observed competitor content patterns; their metrics are not invented. The page-specific primary phrase and nearby long-tail variants are mapped below so future updates do not target the same query blindly.

## Competitor content evidence

The GooseWorks `site-content-catalog` workflow read current sitemaps, RSS, and blog indexes on October 5, 2026. It surfaced these useful patterns:

- **Beacons (`beacons.ai`):** 3,424 sitemap URLs; the catalog classified 3,385 URLs under `/i/blog/`, with Instagram bio ideas, biography, and alternatives themes prominent. This is a large, largely templated footprint, not proof that every variation should become a Grabbin article.
- **Linktree (`linktr.ee`):** its robots file exposed a profile-directory sitemap with 49,106 URLs. The blog index also surfaced posts about creators, podcasts, YouTube, Instagram, and practical topics. The sitemap's large directory is a different page type from original editorial guides.
- **lnk.boo:** 120 URLs in its sitemap; the workflow found 85 blog pages, including link-in-bio tools, personal website builders, musicians, and creator resources.
- **Alllinks (`alllinks.cc`):** 122 URLs discovered; 86 blog pages were cataloged. Titles include link-in-bio ideas, Linktree alternatives, and pages for YouTubers and authors.
- **Sociials (`sociials.com`):** its RSS exposed 15 article URLs, including Instagram/TikTok setup, Linktree comparison, page creation, and common mistakes.
- **TapBee (`tapbee.io`):** the sitemap exposed 12 URLs, but the catalog could not classify them as articles with its blog path rules. A public photographer page was previously identified at `https://tapbee.io/photographers`; this is evidence of vertical positioning, not keyword volume.

**Content decision:** use role-specific articles only where a real task changes the advice (for example, photo permissions and galleries for photographers, release/show destinations for musicians, or archives for writers). Do not produce near-identical articles that only replace the profession name. Add more pages only when Ahrefs, Search Console, or actual reader questions support a distinct page intent.
